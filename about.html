About

Mojdeh Khoshpoor

Product Designer based in Munich, currently at Interhyp Gruppe — one of Germany's largest mortgage platforms.

Background

I design for people who use a product every day to do serious work — mortgage advisors, brokers, internal teams — where clarity, trust and speed matter more than novelty.

I hold a Master of Arts in Interactive Media Systems from Technische Hochschule Augsburg (grade 1.0), with a thesis built in collaboration with Interhyp on collaborative UX for a B2B platform — the full case study is in the password-protected part of my portfolio. Before Munich, I worked as a visual and UI designer in Tehran.

I care about being able to say what a design change actually did, not just that it shipped — which is why I built the UX team's KPI framework at Interhyp. I'm currently looking for my next Product Designer role, ideally one that grows into leading a design team and shaping the process behind it.
